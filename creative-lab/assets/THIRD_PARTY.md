## Demonstration websites (`demos/`)

Kindred Harbor and Vale & Ember are fictional brands built as design demonstrations. Their fonts and photographs are
third-party works used under the licences below; everything else in `demos/` (layouts, copy, icons, styles, scripts) was
written for this repository.

### Photographs

All 29 photographs are from Wikimedia Commons and are dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal**
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Each file's licence, provenance (own work or a passed Commons licence
review) and absence of problem tags was checked individually before download; no Unsplash, Pexels or Pixabay material is used.
Images are cropped, resized and re-encoded (AVIF/WebP) with metadata removed; any further change is noted in the
machine-readable manifests `demos/kindred-harbor/assets.json` and `demos/vale-and-ember/assets.json`, and each demo's
Credits page lists the same information. CC0 does not require attribution; creators are credited as a courtesy.
